# NISR Data Integration & Provenance Guide

## 1. Overview
The National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda (NISR) is the primary source of official statistics in Rwanda. The Hub integrates NISR Labour Force Survey (LFS) and socioeconomic indicators to provide data-informed context for skill development.

## 2. Tracked Indicators & Provenance

### Indicator 1: Employment by Economic Activity — Services Sector
- **Indicator Code**: `NISR-LFS-EMP-SRV`
- **Source**: National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda (NISR)
- **Dataset**: Labour Force Survey (LFS) Annual Report
- **Value**: 38.6%
- **Unit**: % of total employed population
- **Period**: 2023-2024
- **Geography**: Rwanda - National
- **Application Context**: Contextualizes digital services, administration, and customer service skills.

### Indicator 2: Youth Labour Force Participation Rate
- **Indicator Code**: `NISR-LFS-YOUTH-LFPR`
- **Source**: NISR Labour Force Survey
- **Value**: 52.4%
- **Unit**: %
- **Period**: 2023
- **Geography**: Rwanda - National
- **Application Context**: Emphasizes transition from entry-level skills to verified competencies.

### Indicator 3: ICT & Internet Household/Individual Access
- **Indicator Code**: `NISR-ICT-ACCESS`
- **Source**: NISR Statistical Yearbook / RURA Reports
- **Value**: 34.2%
- **Unit**: % individuals accessing broadband
- **Period**: 2023
- **Application Context**: Guides mobile-first, low-bandwidth, and offline-compatible course design.

### Indicator 4: Agriculture Sector Employment Share
- **Indicator Code**: `NISR-LFS-AGRI-SHARE`
- **Source**: NISR Labour Force Survey
- **Value**: 44.8%
- **Unit**: % of total employed population
- **Period**: 2023
- **Application Context**: Supports Agribusiness Fundamentals and Digital Tools for Agriculture pathways.

## 3. Provenance & Attribution Rules
- Every statistic presented in The Hub MUST show `Indicator Name`, `Value`, `Year`, `Unit`, and `Source: NISR`.
- Disclaimers must explicitly state: *"NISR indicators are provided for labour-market context. They do not constitute a guarantee of job placement."*
